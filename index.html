Weekly Biga

  1. Wednesday noon: 15 kg flour + 7.5 kg water + 20 g fresh yeast.
  2. Divide evenly into 3 buckets.
  3. Room temperature for 3 hours, then refrigerate at 3–5°C.
  4. Use one full bucket for Thursday, Friday and Saturday service.

Pizza Dough with Biga

  1. Select the service day and biga condition.
  2. Add ice water, additional flour, fresh yeast and cut-up biga.
  3. Mix until combined, then add salt.
  4. Finish dough at 18°C or slightly above.
  5. Remove, smooth and lightly oil.

Direct Pizza Dough

  1. Add mixing water, flour and calculated fresh yeast. No biga.
  2. Mix until combined, then add salt.
  3. Aim for 21–23°C finished dough.
  4. Portion and ball immediately.
  5. Proof at room temperature. First use from about 3 hours after mixing.

Focaccia

  1. Use 2.5 kg finished dough per focaccia.
  2. Remix with 50 g additional fresh yeast and olive oil.
  3. Place in an oiled, baking-paper-lined gastronorm tray and proof near the oven.
  4. Dimple, oil, salt and proof again.
  5. Bake at approximately 180°C for 35 minutes.

Balling

  1. Weigh dough to the selected ball weight.
  2. Lightly flour trays and shape smooth balls.
  3. Place 12 per tray in a 3 × 4 layout.
  4. Proof at room temperature.
