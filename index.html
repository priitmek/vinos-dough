Weekly Biga

  1. Wednesday around noon: mix 15 kg flour, 7.5 kg water and 20 g fresh yeast (50% hydration).
  2. Divide evenly into three buckets. Each contains approximately 5 kg flour, 2.5 kg water and 6.67 g fresh yeast.
  3. Leave all three at room temperature for approximately 3 hours, then refrigerate at 3–5°C.
  4. Use one bucket for each service, usually around noon Thursday, Friday and Saturday. Check the biga's actual condition before mixing: its age is only a guide.

Pizza Dough with Biga

  1. Select the correct service bucket and assess the biga. Check the suggested fresh yeast and fine-tune if necessary.
  2. Add ice water, additional flour, added fresh yeast and cut-up biga to the mixer.
  3. Mix until well combined and smooth, then add salt.
  4. Continue mixing until the dough reaches at least 18°C. Go slightly higher if the kitchen is cold. Record the finished-dough temperature when comparing batches.
  5. Oil your hands and remove the dough onto the bench, in two lots if necessary. Shape into a large smooth dough ball and lightly oil.

Direct Pizza Dough

  1. Use the direct-method recipe and enter a fresh-yeast quantity suited to your planned proofing time. Do not use the biga-specific yeast recommendation.
  2. Add the calculated ice water, flour and selected fresh yeast to the mixer. No biga is used.
  3. Mix until well combined and smooth, then add salt.
  4. Continue mixing to your target finished-dough temperature. Monitor proofing according to your chosen direct-method schedule.
  5. Oil your hands, remove dough to the bench, shape and lightly oil before portioning.

Focaccia

  1. Weigh 2.5 kg of finished dough for each focaccia.
  2. Remix each 2.5 kg portion with an additional 50 g fresh yeast and a liberal amount of olive oil. This is separate from the calculator's added fresh yeast.
  3. Place on a baking-paper-lined, oiled gastronorm tray. Proof near the oven, turning until it spreads across most of the tray.
  4. Prevent a crust forming; add olive oil as needed. Dimple, add sea salt flakes and proof again.
  5. Bake at approximately 180°C for 35 minutes.

Balling

  1. While the focaccia proofs, weigh remaining dough into the selected ball weight.
  2. Lightly flour trays. Shape smooth dough balls and place 12 per tray in a 3 × 4 layout.
  3. Proof at room temperature. Monitor more closely with a very active or older biga, especially on warm days.
