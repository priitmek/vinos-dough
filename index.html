Pizza Dough

  1. Add ice water, flour, fresh yeast and the cut-up biga to the mixer.
  2. Mix until well combined and the dough looks smooth.
  3. Add the salt.
  4. Continue mixing until the dough reaches at least 18°C. Go slightly higher if the kitchen is cold.
  5. Oil your hands and pull the dough onto the bench. With a large batch, remove it in two lots if needed.
  6. Shape into one large smooth dough ball and lightly oil it for easier handling.

Focaccia

  1. Weigh 2.5 kg of dough for each focaccia.
  2. Return each 2.5 kg focaccia portion to the mixer with an extra 50 g fresh yeast and a liberal amount of olive oil. This yeast is separate from the base recipe.
  3. Place on a gastronorm tray lined with baking paper and coat with olive oil.
  4. Proof near the oven, turning until it has spread across most of the tray.
  5. Do not allow a crust to form. Add more olive oil if needed.
  6. Press dimples into the dough, sprinkle with sea salt flakes and proof a second time.
  7. Bake at approximately 180°C for 35 minutes.

Balling

  1. While the focaccia is proofing, weigh the remaining dough into the selected ball weight.
  2. Dust the dough trays lightly with flour.
  3. Ball into smooth dough balls.
  4. Place 12 per tray in a 3 × 4 layout.
  5. Leave to proof at room temperature.
House formula: 1 × 7.5 kg biga bucket at 50% hydration, 70% default target hydration, 2.5% salt, temperature-adjusted fresh yeast and experimental humidity-adjusted working hydration.
